# HFS
Hand Flight Simulator

The Hand Flight Simulator is a simulation to practice your coordination through the libraries mainly of mediapipe and openpose which tracks your body and allows you to navigate with your hands. There is also other gamemodes like multiple maps, multiple aero vehicles, an ai that learns by faliure and more. More on the ai is that you can verse it yourself. There are also other gamemodes that you can play, and you can train the ai/evolve the Neural Network so it can fly better. I suggest not the swift plane because it's too fast with the detailed high obstacle maps;-;, that's one example where some excercises/maps with the planes get pretty difficult. You can also change how openpose tracks you by changing the setting in the bottom or the sensitivity of the tracking as well! And you can boost by clenching your fist. We ran into a little bit of a problem that the openpose libraries and the graphics were taking up too much resources on any computer for a website so we reduced the libraries and only kept the main ones at certain times.

## Features

- Hand-tracked flight using MediaPipe and OpenPose
- Multiple game modes and maps
- Multiple aircraft to choose from
- AI that learns from failure and improves over time
- Player-vs-AI gameplay and neural network training
- Adjustable tracking sensitivity and settings
- Boost mechanic by clenching your fist
- Optimized performance to reduce heavy resource usage

## How to Play

Start the simulator and use your body movements to control the aircraft with your hands. Navigate through different maps, choose your preferred aircraft, and explore the available game modes. As you play, you can improve your coordination and reaction time while learning how different aircraft handle in more difficult obstacle-heavy environments.

## Controls

- Use your hands and body movement to steer and navigate
- Adjust OpenPose tracking settings from the bottom controls or change sensitivity
- Use a fist-clench to boost your aircraft
- Choose between different maps and airplanes depending on the challenge
- Be careful with faster aircraft like the Swift plane, especially on difficult maps with many obstacles

## AI and Training

The AI in Hand Flight Simulator learns from failure and can evolve over time as it improves. You can also train and evolve the Neural Network so it becomes better at flying. There are gameplay modes where you can challenge the AI yourself, helping you practice skill and strategy while improving the system's performance.

## Performance Notes

We ran into a problem where the OpenPose libraries and graphics were using too much resources on many computers, especially for a web-based experience. To address this, we reduced the libraries and kept only the essential ones active at the right times so the game remains more efficient while still preserving the main experience.
